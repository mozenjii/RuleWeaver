# R2 - #17 Cross-border business, tax and payments advisor

## 1. Does anyone pay?
Not for what exists: all 20 said "would not buy today", and the verified facts worsen it (shipped SNAP parameters return "unknown", no faults are ever seeded, the NOTICE's "named reviewer" is false). Conditional buyers I find most credible, since they can contract with a Pakistani sole proprietor without a US procurement wall:
- #09 UK calculator vendor: GBP 8-15k fixed fee, Universal Credit pilot. Small, B2B, under tender thresholds, one language.
- #02 benefits nonprofit: USD 10-25k, one state, buys fast.
- #12 Big-4 partner: GBP 20-50k, only as subcontractor with their reviewers. A channel, not a customer.
All prices are simulated, not market data. I distrust #01, #03 and #04 (USD 50-150k a year): 6-12 month onboarding for a solo offshore vendor with a day job.

## 2. Disagreements
- #06, #15, #18 (govtech, privacy counsel, VC): "form a US LLC first". It fixes none of their blockers. Offshore work-location clauses, Pub 1075 and the missing credentialed signer survive a Delaware shell run from Pakistan. It adds EIN, Form 5472 and bank KYC friction, and nobody checked whether a Pakistani resident may hold a foreign company (SBP rules; uncertain). Form one when a buyer's LOI demands a US counterparty.
- #16 (security): pentest USD 5-20k plus insurance USD 2-8k a year before the first paid pilot. Add #15's USD 5-15k legal setup and you spend roughly USD 12-43k against a USD 5-25k fee: a negative-margin first deal.
- #19 and #18: "VAT handling" and "W-8BEN-E". A sole proprietor files W-8BEN, and B2B VAT is reverse charge with no registration.

Changed my mind: #13 and #14 showed self-hosting is clean only with zero data access, so support must be a redacted bundle. #15 plus the verified facts showed that an MSA warranty of "reviewed by a named reviewer" is false today and a deceptive-claims risk; strike it.

## 3. Attacking the consensus
"Entity, insurance, security pack, credentialed reviewer first" is sequenced backwards and unpriced. Nobody budgeted the reviewer: at an assumed USD 150/hour and 80 hours, USD 12k of a USD 10-25k pilot goes to them. Insurance may be unobtainable for a Pakistan-based sole proprietor (#16 says so). Payment terms are ignored: #06 expects net 45-60 and 5-10% retainage, which a salaried founder must float while wires take days. Correct order: free items (employer release), then a signed LOI, then paid compliance funded by the pilot deposit.

## 4. Final call
- Buyer: UK benefits calculator vendor or charity (#09), Universal Credit core.
- Delivery: self-hosted, zero data access, fixed-fee pilot, reviewer subcontracted and passed through at cost.
- Why, from my lane: UK B2B services are reverse-charged, with no VAT registration; the UK is 4-5 hours from PKT, the US East 9-10; legislation.gov.uk is open, structured and single-language.
- Unhidden risk: the fixed April uprating deadline against a day job (#09's own deal-breaker). Fallback: #12 as channel.
- Stop: building plumbing (hosted API, metering, OpenFisca polish, SNAP).

Round-1 verdict: unchanged on "premature"; changed on market (UK first, not US) and on no US LLC until a buyer asks.
